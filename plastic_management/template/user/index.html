Utilize Plastic Recycling Company

Utilize Company, based in New York, specializes in plastic recycling and commercial plastics recycling. Our main specialty is in the purchase and resale of plastic materials in the secondary market. Utilize has built a reputation on a pioneering spirit for developing new markets and technologies to meet tomorrow’s plastic scrap recycling demands. We recycle all grades of plastic, and purchase raw plastic scrap in any shape or form, regrind and virgin resin. We offer toll grinding services and specialize in used plastic pallets, bins, totes, and boxes. We transform your plastic scrap and plastic film into useful material that can be used to make a huge variety of products, from furniture to windows, flooring, and insulation.
We believe plastic recycling is an important and needed service, and we are proud to call ourselves “the best in the industry”.
